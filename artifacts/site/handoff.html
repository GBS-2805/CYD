Rules

  • One site. Production is the only public address; the preview stays saved and offline.
  • Render before deploy. A UI change gets a public labelled link before commit, push or deploy.
  • The APK is exact. The site serves the bytes in android-companion/dist/; the hash is the check.
  • The deck is separate. The APK carries the sketch text, not a flash. ArduinoDroid is the proven path.
  • No device, no claim. A JVM capture is not a phone screenshot; label hardware as unverified until it runs.
  • Fix the cause. A red gate names drift; it is fixed, never skipped.

Words

What the page means, in the order you meet it.

APK
The installable Android package. It contains the app and the bundled sketch text, not the deck's flashed firmware.
Debug build
The version here is signed with the project key in the tree. Installable, reproducible, not a store build.
SHA-256
A fingerprint of the file's exact bytes. Compare it with the published hash; one changed byte changes the hash.
Cutout / inset
The camera hole or notch, and the padding the app adds so words and controls stay outside it and the system bars.
Status bar icons
The clock, signal and battery. On Android 15+ they are drawn in the band's ink because the band is the colour behind them.
Capture
The app's own views drawn by Android's renderer on a JVM. It proves arithmetic, not a device's fonts or camera hole.
Fast gate
./check.sh: the checks a commit must pass before it lands. A red step is fixed, not waived.
Production / preview
The public address versus the saved copy kept offline. Off-site has one public environment: production.
Phone deck / Spotify deck
The two CYD sketches, version 1.0.1 and 1.0.0. They are separate from the app and each needs its own flash.
ArduinoDroid
The Android app that compiles and uploads a sketch over OTG. It is the proven deck path; the companion app's own uploader is not.
BLE / Phone Link
The Bluetooth service the phone deck exposes; one deck is linked at a time.
Mirror
The phone's media line written to the deck about once a second. The deck draws what it was told; it does not fetch Spotify itself.
Tap round-trip
A deck chip sends a play/pause notify to the phone. The next mirror line, not the lit chip, proves the action ran.
Stale bond
A Bluetooth pairing the phone or deck no longer agrees on. Both sides try to forget it and link again.
Walk-away
Leaving range and returning. The expected line is a reconnect without a fresh scan; the log decides whether it worked.
